### 1. Intended Purpose
This software system, titled **"AI Based Brain Tumor Detection and Localization for Surgical Planning"**, is developed solely as an **academic engineering research prototype** for a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) Computer Engineering final-year capstone project.

### 2. Not a Medical Diagnostic Device
* **THIS SYSTEM IS NOT CERTIFIED AS A MEDICAL DEVICE** under FDA (U.S. Food and Drug Administration), CE-MDR (European Union Medical Device Regulation), CDSCO (Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation, India), or any other regulatory health authority.
* The system is **NOT** designed, validated, or certified to provide primary medical diagnoses, definitive surgical margins, or independent patient triage.
* Outputs produced by the AI models, algorithms, segmentation masks, bounding boxes, size estimations, or volumetric calculations must **never** be used as a standalone or primary basis for diagnostic, surgical, or therapeutic decisions.

### 3. Decision-Support Role
* If deployed in experimental clinical observation, this software is strictly categorized as a **Computer-Aided Detection / Decision-Support System (CADe/CADx)**.
* Any visualizations or metrics are intended solely to assist licensed neurosurgeons and board-certified radiologists by providing secondary analytical references.
* Final medical authority, anatomical verification, tumor margin delineation, and surgical trajectories remain exclusively with the attending licensed medical professionals.
